Our Services

Help that meets families where they are.

From the first days after arrival to the path toward citizenship and stable work, we offer practical, culturally-grounded support at every stage.

Refugee & Newcomer Support

Welcome orientation and one-on-one guidance to help newly arrived families understand housing, healthcare, schools, transportation, and daily life in Utica.

Interpretation & Translation

Maay Maay, Somali, and English language assistance for medical appointments, school meetings, benefits offices, and important documents.

Education, ESL & Citizenship

English language learning, homework help, and citizenship-test preparation that build the skills families need to succeed in school and civic life.

Youth & Family Programs

After-school activities, mentoring, and family support that help children adjust, stay engaged in school, and grow into confident young adults.

Employment & Self-Sufficiency

Job-readiness help, connections to local employers and training, and guidance through benefits and paperwork on the road to independence.

Community Connection

Gatherings and civic engagement that keep families connected to one another and woven into the wider Utica community.

Service availability may vary. Please contact us to learn what support is currently offered and how we can help your family.
