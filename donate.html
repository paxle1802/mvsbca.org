Donate

Help a refugee family find their footing.

Your tax-deductible gift puts interpreters in clinics, teachers in classrooms, and mentors beside young people who are starting over in Utica.

Your Impact

Where your gift goes

  • Interpretation so families can be understood at the doctor, school, and benefits office.
  • English & citizenship classes that open doors to work and belonging.
  • Youth programs that keep children engaged and supported.
  • Direct guidance for families navigating life in a new country.

Every contribution — large or small — strengthens the welcome we can offer.
